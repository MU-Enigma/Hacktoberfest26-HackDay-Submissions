## Problem

Money management is a serious problem among university students.

## Solution

We built a money tracking app namely Wispend that can track all your spendings and categorize it. It can suggest you safe spending limit for everyday use and can help you manage your monthly spendings.

## Technologies used

- Google AI studio, Claude

## How to run

1. Import your statement pdf from Phonepe
2. Enter the Budget, savings limit which will give you Safe spending cap to save the amount.
3. You can check your past transactions that you can compare it with the monthly safe spending limit.
4. It can give you a descriptive data of your spendings and help you build saving habits.
